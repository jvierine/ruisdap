Why the resolution looks different

Juha's Rust analyses and Historical fit one window per zenith antenna cycle, about 190 s, on 4.5 km range cells (240 cells, matching the 30 µs code bauds), averaged over 6 cells (about 27 km) at 300–700 km before the fit. The isr_analysis runs fit 60 s windows, about three per zenith cycle (219 fits on 8 April 11–23 UTC against 145 for the whole day), on 9 km gates (116 gates of 60 µs), averaged over 7 gates (about 63 km) at 300–700 km. So the Rust maps have columns about three times wider and finer range structure, and their longer windows make the topside less noisy; differences between the two sides mix the satellite method with this averaging. Madrigal's analysed fits keep their own resolution: mostly 3 min windows (with some 10 s records), 18 km cells in mode 115 and 4.5 km in mode 97.

Quality flags

“Hide flagged gates” blanks the gates each analysis marks as unreliable, each side by its own test: for the isr_analysis runs the fit_quality.py flags FAILED (a parameter or its error is not finite), BOUND (the fit ends at a limit of its search) and NOISY (relative error of Ne or Te above 0.5); for Juha's native runs any fit_status other than “converged” (in those runs most gates end at a parameter boundary, so much is hidden); Historical carries no flags and is shown in full.
