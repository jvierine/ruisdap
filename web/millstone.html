Definition and units

The current empirical constant has units \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-3}\,K^{-1}\,km^{-2}}\).

\[p=\frac{A_0(1+T_e/T_i)r^2}{P_{\rm tx}},\qquad A_0\;[\mathrm K],\quad r\;[\mathrm{km}],\quad P_{\rm tx}\;[\mathrm W].\]

Recorded transmit power and receiver gain have already been removed from this proxy.

\[Cp=\frac{n_e(1+T_e/T_i)}{(1+q)(1+q+T_e/T_i)},\qquad q=k^2\lambda_D^2,\qquad q\to0:\;n_e=Cp.\]

Plasma frequency supplies density after the Bohm–Gross thermal correction. The estimate is the median within each ion fit, followed by the median across independent fits. Scatter is the scaled median absolute deviation between fits; frequency, temperature and systematic errors are additional.

The native Rust decoder removes pulse-energy and range-cell normalization in each measurement row: \[E_s=\sum_j |u_j|^2,\qquad \Delta r={c\,d\over2f_s},\qquad A_{\rm physical}=E_s\,\Delta r\,A_{\rm unit\ energy}.\] Here \(u_j\) is the transmit waveform at unit peak power, \(d\) is the range decimation, and \(\Delta r\) is in km. Each pulse retains its own energy for mixed codes. A fixed-code legacy constant converts as \(C_{\rm native}=C_{\rm legacy}E_s\Delta r\), with units \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-3}\,K^{-1}\,km^{-1}}\). The 30 µs, 100 µs and mixed-code forward/inverse checks use the actual decoder operations.

The displayed historical data retain their recorded normalization. The present constant includes the effective pulse and decoder range response. The physical baud range is \(\Delta r_b=c\tau_b/2\): 10, 30 and 100 µs correspond to 1.499, 4.497 and 14.990 km. The effective scattering volume must use the decoded power ambiguity response, including receive filtering and code normalization. The shortest baud alone does not determine it for mixed codes. Constants from different code lengths cannot yet be pooled.

Processing access

Density is recomputed from the reviewed magic constant C with the finite-Debye ion-power correction. Source Ti, Te and velocity remain fixed; this is not a new ACF fit.
