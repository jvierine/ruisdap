Plasma-line calibration

The selected range supplies a candidate ion-line gate. Review the correspondence; a broad pulse response does not uniquely locate a plasma line. The calculation uses the fitted temperatures, a Bohm–Gross thermal correction and the finite-Debye ion-power denominator. Both frequency signs give the same cold-plasma density; the receiver sign is retained.
