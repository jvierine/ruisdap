Calculation and limitations
Uses the one-minute Te, Ti and Ne fit at the chosen altitude. Field-aligned unmagnetized Bohm–Gross thermal correction: ωpe² = (2πf)² − 3k²kBTe/me, k = 4πfradar/c. No magnetic-field or electron-drift correction. Finite-Debye ion power is proportional to Ne / [(1+q)(1+q+Te/Ti)], q = k²λD². The estimated magic constant is C = C_input × predicted 10 µs power / measured 10 µs power. The fitted line uses measured raw Ne divided by C_input, so its slope and uncertainty directly estimate C.
Measured 10 µs raw Ne is converted with the experiment’s spectral response, including partial gates, and the one-minute fitted spectral shape evaluated at the plasma-line density. The conversion includes Doppler and collisional decorrelation. Samples are averaged over the requested time/altitude window using complete available time bins; choose a finer plasma resolution for finer averaging. The inference inherits fit, composition and range-response errors. Bohm–Gross is a long-wavelength approximation: q ≥ 0.1 is flagged. Frequency mapping remains under validation. Nonphysical thermal corrections are rejected. Fit uncertainties are shown; calibration-factor uncertainty is not yet propagated. Saved points persist in this browser; export them for a portable record. Points can be excluded individually. The result does not automatically rescale data or change server processing.
