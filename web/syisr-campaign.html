Analysis and calibration

All 63 files and 5,505,066 pulses were screened before averaging. Products use only voltage pairs with both samples retained. All 41 lags, from 0 to 400 µs, are used. Each lag retains its own range/receiver ambiguity in the forward model; these are not equal-ambiguity ACFs. Curves below show measured products at one receive sample, not an independent local ACF.

Continuous profile parameters have 150 km knot spacing with 25 km range quadrature. Electron response is magnetized using IGRF and each beam’s remote scattering geometry. The receiver response uses a short-lag tapered noise reference, with a residual background amplitude profiled. Error weights take the larger of propagated pulse SEM and between-dwell variability; full lag/range covariance and receiver-response uncertainty are not marginalized. He⁺ maps are conditional joint-fit point estimates, not independently established He⁺ detections. Shared ion temperature is assumed for H⁺, He⁺ and O⁺. The physical velocity sign needs independent validation. A prescribed Debye-shape density of 10¹¹ m⁻³ is not a measurement of Ne. Range-dependent power remains in ADU² until system calibration is supplied.

The maps use the common RUISDAP Rust/WASM/WebGL renderer and rustplotlib navigation and colour controls. Click a colour bar to adjust its limits. Select a pixel for diagnostics. The first and last half-hour cells contain partial coverage. No results are substituted from supplied SYISR L2.
